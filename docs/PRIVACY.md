# Spelunkr TV Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Published by OnePatchDown, LLC.

The Apple TV app has no account, analytics, or tracking. Favorites stay on your Apple TV. Remove them in the guide or delete the app to clear its local data.

The app requests listings, artwork, and streams directly from Spelunkr and the Internet Archive. These providers receive your IP address and ordinary request details, which they handle under their own policies. OnePatchDown does not operate a server that receives these requests or stores your viewing activity.

For privacy questions, open an issue at [github.com/fragmede/spelunkr-tv/issues](https://github.com/fragmede/spelunkr-tv/issues). Issues are public. Keep personal information out of them.
